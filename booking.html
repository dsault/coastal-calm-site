If you would rather inquire first, call or message us. We will confirm availability by phone, then in writing. The confirmation will name the rate sold (Plan Ahead, Opening Special, flexible, Senior/PWD, or monthly) and the cancel rule that goes with it. Direct bookings are confirmed by the resort and paid at the resort. Once a direct booking is confirmed, Plan Ahead and other non-refundable rates (including the Opening Special) are not cash-refundable. Bookings made on Agoda follow Agoda's payment terms. Flexible bookings can be cancelled free until 3:00 PM, three days before check-in. After that, the full stay is charged. Peak dates need two nights; see the peak calendar. A no-show is charged in full. Breakfast is ₱250 per person per night, added at booking, and is not discounted. Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM. Late arrivals after 10:00 PM can be arranged in advance by contacting the resort. No smoking in rooms (₱5,000 cleaning fee if smoke is found); no pets; no dried fish in rooms.

The non-refundable Plan Ahead rate (10% off) is bookable any time. For direct bookings, payment is due at the resort, and that rate is not cash-refundable once confirmed. Bookings made on Agoda follow Agoda's payment terms. The flexible rate is about 10% higher. Standard rooms have a December Opening Special of ₱1,500 non-refundable and ₱1,650 flexible for stays through 31 December 2026, except 24, 25 and 31 December, which use regular rates. From 1 January 2027 the Opening Special is ₱2,000 non-refundable and ₱2,200 flexible, bookable through 31 January 2027, for stays through 31 March 2027 except peak dates.
