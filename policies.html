Rules & Policies

A quiet resort by the water works when everyone keeps the same promises. A confirmed reservation is acceptance of these rules. Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM.

No smoking in rooms

No smoking in guest rooms. Smoking is only allowed in designated outdoor areas. This includes vapes. If smoke is found in a room we will charge for deep cleaning and may end the stay.

No pets

Pets are not permitted anywhere on the property, including rooms, balconies, decks, and grounds. Please do not arrive with animals — we cannot make exceptions.

Cancellation

A credit card is required to hold any reservation; payment is due on arrival. Posted rates are the Plan Ahead rate (10% off): book 30 or more days ahead with a non-refundable 50% deposit. Flexible-rate bookings can be cancelled free until 7 days before arrival. Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM.

Resort policies

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out depends on the day’s occupancy — ask when you book.

Guest risk & damage

Guests accept the natural risks of an oceanfront stay (wet floors, sea conditions, pool) and agree to cover damage to rooms, furniture, and grounds beyond ordinary wear.

Resort rules

A confirmed booking is acceptance of these policies, the cancellation schedule, and the posted resort rules at the property.

Weather & ferries

Force majeure, weather, and ferry cancellations: any non-refundable amount is held as credit, valid for 12 months.

Medical / family emergency

Considered case-by-case with documentation. Non-refundable amounts may be converted to 12-month credit.

Included vs extra

In the room rate

Stay for two adults, children under 5 free, pool and jacuzzi, ocean swimming, kids’ play, coffee/tea, kettle, hair dryer, bathrobes, slippers, hot shower, parking, solar-powered stay.

At a charge

Karaoke, pool tables, restaurant/lounge, entertainment, four 22 kW EV fast chargers, bamboo picnic houses. Extra adult ₱500/night · extra child 5+ ₱300/night · extra bed ₱300/night · extra pillow ₱100 · port transfer ₱500 per person, each way.
