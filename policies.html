Rules & Policies

A quiet house by the water works when everyone keeps the same promises. A confirmed reservation is acceptance of these rules.

Smoke-free resort

No smoking in guest rooms. No smoking in any public area — restaurant, lounge, decks, pool, event tent, corridors, or grounds. This includes vapes. If smoke is found in a room we will charge for deep cleaning and may end the stay.

No pets

Pets are not permitted anywhere on the property, including rooms, balconies, decks, and grounds. Please do not arrive with animals — we cannot make exceptions.

Cancellation

A credit card is required to hold any reservation. Base-rate bookings can be cancelled free until 7 days before arrival. The plan-ahead discount requires a non-refundable 50% deposit.

House policies

Guest risk & damage

Guests accept the natural risks of an oceanfront stay (wet floors, sea conditions, pool) and agree to cover damage to rooms, furniture, and grounds beyond ordinary wear.

Resort rules

A confirmed booking is acceptance of these policies, the cancellation schedule, and the posted house rules at the property.

Weather & ferries

Force majeure, weather, and ferry cancellations: any non-refundable amount is held as credit, valid for 12 months.

Medical / family emergency

Considered case-by-case with documentation. Non-refundable amounts may be converted to 12-month credit.

Included vs extra

In the room rate

Stay for two adults, children under 5 free, pool and jacuzzi, ocean swimming, kids’ play, coffee/tea, kettle, hair dryer, bathrobes, slippers, hot shower, parking, solar-powered stay.

At a charge

Karaoke, pool tables, restaurant/lounge, entertainment, four 22 kW EV fast chargers, bamboo picnic houses. Extra adult ₱500/night · extra child 5+ ₱300/night · extra bed ₱300/night · extra pillow ₱100 · port transfer ₱500 per person, each way.
