Rules & Policies

A quiet resort by the water works when everyone keeps the same promises. A confirmed reservation is acceptance of these rules. Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM.

No smoking in rooms

No smoking in guest rooms. Smoking is only allowed in designated outdoor areas. This includes vapes. If smoke is found in a room we charge a ₱5,000 deep-cleaning fee to the card on file and may end the stay.

No pets

Pets are not permitted anywhere on the property, including rooms, balconies, decks, and grounds. Please do not arrive with animals — we cannot make exceptions. If you travel with a trained assistance animal, contact us before you book.

Cancellation

A credit card is required to hold every reservation. Posted rates are the Plan Ahead rate (10% off): book 15 or more days ahead. Fifty percent of the quoted total (room plus extras) is charged at confirmation and is not cash-refundable; the remaining balance is due on arrival. Flexible-rate bookings can be cancelled free until three days before arrival; after that two nights are charged if you cancel (or the whole stay if shorter). A no-show is charged in full. Ferry disruption or documented health/hardship converts cancellation fees to 12-month credit. Peak dates on the calendar below need two nights. Check-in 3:00 PM, check-out 11:00 AM.

Resort policies

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out depends on the day’s occupancy — ask when you book.

Guest risk & damage

Guests accept the natural risks of an oceanfront stay (wet floors, sea conditions, pool) and agree to cover damage to rooms, furniture, and grounds beyond ordinary wear. Repair or replacement is billed to the card on file.

Resort rules

A confirmed booking is acceptance of these policies, the cancellation schedule, and the posted resort rules at the property.

Weather & ferries

Ferry disruption (the sailing is cancelled or does not operate) and documented health or hardship: no cash refunds, but cancellation fees — including a Plan Ahead deposit and the two-night late-cancel charge — are held as credit for 12 months, same guest name, one stay. A missed ferry is not disruption.

Medical / family emergency

Documented health or hardship: considered by the manager with documents. Qualifying cancellation fees — including a Plan Ahead deposit and the two-night late-cancel charge — are converted to the same 12-month credit. No cash refunds.

Included vs extra

In the room rate

Stay for two adults, children under 5 free when sharing the existing bed, pool and jacuzzi, ocean swimming, kids’ play, coffee/tea, kettle, hair dryer, bathrobes, slippers, hot shower, parking, solar-powered stay.

At a charge

Karaoke, pool tables, restaurant/lounge, entertainment, four 22 kW EV fast chargers, bamboo picnic houses. Extra adult ₱500/night · extra child 5+ ₱300/night · extra bed ₱300/night (a child 5+ who needs a bed is charged both) · extra pillow ₱100 / stay · port transfer ₱500 per person, each way.
