Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

D&D Coastal Calm Resort (“we”, “the resort”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we use it, and how you can ask us about it. We follow the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Who we are

D&D Coastal Calm Resort, Mahaba Road, Mercedes, Poro, Camotes Islands, Cebu, Philippines. Privacy questions: stay@coastal-calm.com or +63 960 911 3729. See Contact.

What we collect

  • Booking details — name, dates, room type, guest count, phone, email, and payment details needed to hold or charge a stay. Most room reservations are made through our booking partner, Letsbook.
  • Inquiries — name, email, phone, and the message you send by form, email, Facebook, or WhatsApp (events, transfers, group stays).
  • Stay records — confirmation notes, rate sold, cancellation credits, and house-rule incidents (for example a smoking fee) so we can run the property fairly.
  • Website use — this site is static. We do not run our own advertising cookies. Your browser and our host (Cloudflare) may log standard technical data such as IP address, pages viewed, and device type to keep the site online and secure.

How we use it

  • To confirm, change, or cancel a reservation and to take the deposit or balance described in our Policies.
  • To answer questions, arrange port transfers, and plan events.
  • To meet legal, tax, and safety obligations.
  • To improve the website and prevent abuse.

We do not sell guest lists or browsing data. We do not send marketing email unless you asked us to.

Who we share it with

  • Letsbook — room bookings and payments on letsbook.me are processed under Letsbook’s own terms and privacy notice. We receive the reservation details we need to host you.
  • Payment processors used by Letsbook or by our bank when we charge a card on file.
  • Hosting and security — Cloudflare and related infrastructure that serve this website.
  • Authorities if the law requires it, or to protect guests and staff.

Facebook and YouTube are separate services. If you contact us there, their rules apply to that conversation as well as ours.

How long we keep it

Reservation and billing records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules in the Philippines require, then deleted or archived with access limited. Inquiry messages we no longer need are removed. Cancellation credits are kept until they expire (12 months from issue) plus a short admin period.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or to limit how we use it, subject to records we must keep for law or an active stay. Write to stay@coastal-calm.com and name the stay or email you used. You may also contact the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children for marketing. Guest names and ages may appear on a booking so we can apply occupancy and meal-plan rules (children under 5 stay free when sharing the existing bed).

Photos on this site

Photographs and video on this website show the resort. They are not guest portraits collected from you. Do not post other people’s images from the property in a way that identifies them without their consent.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date at the top is the latest version. Questions: Contact.
