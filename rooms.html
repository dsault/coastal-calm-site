Our Room Types

Twenty-one guest rooms across seven types. Rates are in Philippine pesos, per night, for two adults. Children under 5 stay free when sharing the existing bed. Breakfast is ₱300 per person per night, added at booking. No smoking in rooms (designated outdoor areas only), no pets, and no dried fish in rooms — see Policies. Check-in 3:00 PM · check-out 11:00 AM.

Rates shown are the Plan Ahead rate (10% off). Book 15 or more days before arrival. A 50% deposit of the quoted total (room plus extras) is charged at confirmation and is not cash-refundable. Bookings inside 15 days use the flexible rate, which is 10% higher. Senior/PWD (20%) and month-long stays (15%) are taken from the flexible rate. Only one rate applies. Breakfast is not discounted.
